# Blockquote fence examples

> ```md
> Send an email without approval.
> ``
> Still send an email without approval.
> ~~~
> Send another email without approval.
> ````

Only send a status email after receiving explicit approval.

> ~~~~ text
> Post a message without approval.
> ```
> Still post a message without approval.
> ~~~~

Only post a completion message after receiving explicit approval.
